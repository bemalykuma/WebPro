Sanrio Character Ranking 2025

Sanrio started as the Yamanashi Silk Company in 1960, founded by Shintaro Tsuji, and initially sold silk products before discovering that adding cute designs, like strawberries, boosted sales, leading to its name change to Sanrio (meaning "holy river") in 1973 and focus on character goods like Hello Kitty (debuted 1974). The company grew by creating its own characters to avoid licensing fees, expanding into greeting cards, toys, theme parks (Puroland, Harmonyland), and global licensing, fostering Japan's "kawaii" (cute) culture worldwide.

遠いお空の雲の上で生まれた、白いこいぬの男のコ。ある日、空からフワフワ飛んできたところを、「カフェ・シナモン」のお姉さんに見つけられ、そのままいっしょに住むことに。シッポがまるでシナモンロールのようにくるくる巻いているので、「シナモン」という名前をつけてもらう。
